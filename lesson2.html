🗣 Гап Занед

💬 Суҳбат

Ali: This is my family.

John: Who is this?

Ali: This is my mother.

John: Do you have a brother?

Ali: Yes. I have one brother.

📖 Грамматика

This is... → This is my mother.

I have... → I have one brother.

Who is this? → Пурсидани шахс.

📖 Хонед

My name is Ali. I have a small family. I have one mother, one father, one brother, and two sisters.
