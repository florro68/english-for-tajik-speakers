Ҳадафи дарс

Тоҷикӣ: Дар бораи нақлиёт суҳбат кардан, билет харидан ва вақти рафту омадро пурсидан.

English: Talk about transportation, buy a ticket, and ask about departure and arrival times.

Гӯш кунед, баъд матнро бинед

Аввал танҳо гӯш кунед. Пас ба савол ҷавоб диҳед.

What time does the bus leave?

Passenger: Good morning. I would like one ticket to Dushanbe, please.
Clerk: One-way or return?
Passenger: One-way, please.
Clerk: The bus leaves at nine thirty.
Passenger: What time does it arrive?
Clerk: At twelve o’clock.
Passenger: How much is the ticket?
Clerk: It is twenty dollars.

Грамматика

How do you go to work? I go by bus.

What time does the train leave? It leaves at seven.

I would like one ticket to Riga.

Use by with transport: by bus, by train, by taxi. Say on foot when you walk.
